Theodore and Samantha-Jane Cats were born in Sonoma County where I adopted Theo, and then in 6 months he adopted Sam. They moved from Santa Rosa, to Santa Cruz, to Tucson, to Los Angeles, to Santa Cruz again. They made a lot of great friends along the way being social kitties. I made this website so that all the people that have come to know Theodore and Samantha can see them anytime. They now rest under the shade of a Japanese maple for all time at 430 Windham St. Santa Cruz, CA 95062
