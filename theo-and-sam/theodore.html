Theodore, (A.K.A Mr. Fuzzy Pants, Theo)
Passed away on 3/23/15 he was just about 20 years old

He was doing really great the past couple of weeks. We are very grateful for the extra care and support that he received from our House Call Veterinarian Dr. Maria Kuty. About a month or so ago he was in pretty bad shape, but he was able to turn his lights back on and power through it. This last Sunday I spent all day with him since I noticed that he was starting to trip again. When I came home Monday he seemed happy but couldn't walk right. I saw that he was going down hill super fast, and I knew that this was going to be my last day with him. The morning was little rough, but I got him medicated and he was able to relax and just rest, till the afternoon when he went to sleep for forever.

The first two pictures are of Theo on Sunday. The last one is of him in the afternoon resting, where I then buried him. He is right next to a Japanese Maple that my cousins from Sonoma County gave me. Samantha-Jane, his adopted sister, is doing well since she has had time to adjust to Theo be less active. Samantha got to see Theo asleep forever so she could understand.

Theodore lived a long and happy life.
