Samantha-Jane, (A.K.A Sammy, Sammers, Sam)
Passed away on 12/4/16 she was just about 21 years old

Samantha was one tough kitty. Her mom died when she was kitten. She ran away from her home twice to be with Theodore, so he adopted her. She was 2 to 3 pounds lighter and smaller than Theo but wouldn't run from a tussle. She once pinned a big brutish bully cat weighting 5 more pounds than her, just to make sure he knew that she wasn't meant to be picked on. She stuck by Theodore till the end. Her thyroid was messed up a bit when she was 12 and she just took her pill and kept on going. Her kidneys starting going in her very late teens and she just peed on that idea of slowing her down. About a year ago she became diabetic and that was a hiccup, but as long as she had extra care she kept going! Unfortunately while she kicked some furry tails she developed cancer. Even with that she gave a good go of sticking around, being a happy cat a bit longer, making sure she had as much time as she could comfortably, till it was time to move on and join Theo and my Dad who are very happy to take care of her once again. Again I am very grateful for the extra care and support that she received from our House Call Veterinarian Dr. Maria Kuty

Samantha lived a long and happy life.
