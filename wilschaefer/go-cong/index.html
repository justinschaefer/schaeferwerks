Go Cong, "Little Success"

SP4 WIL SCHAEFER · 45TH MED DET · DATE UNCONFIRMED

Nearly every frame in this tray is shot from the air — a helicopter-borne combat assault into Go Cong, filmed from his own aircraft as it happened: the approach, the landing zone, ground troops moving through it, and the flight home. Slides 43–46 were never scanned, or didn't survive.

#24

#26
"Church In Town"

A single-spire church, Gothic style, visible aerially over the town. Checked against known Gò Công churches — couldn't pin down the specific building, but confirmed something real: Gò Công was an actual French, then South Vietnamese, administrative province (1900–1976), matching his era exactly, not just a village name.

#27
"Soccer Field"

This is more specific than "an open clearing" — a real stadium, with a full grandstand of spectators watching a UH-1 helicopter land on the pitch. A French colonial-style building with a red tile roof stands behind the trees. "Army" markings are visible on the tail boom; the specific serial isn't cleanly legible.

#28
"Firing Squad Anyone?"

#29
