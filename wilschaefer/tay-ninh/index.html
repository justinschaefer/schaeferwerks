Tay Ninh, "Shroud"

SP4 WIL SCHAEFER · 45TH MED DET · EST. EARLY 1964

One stop, documented start to finish — the airfield, the aircraft, the aid tent he worked out of, the town around it, and Núi Bà Đen standing over all of it. Slide 87 was never scanned, or didn't survive; everything else from this stay is here, in the order he numbered it.

#77
Title card

Opens the tray: "Tay Ninh, 'Shroud.'" Handwritten, blue ground.

#78
"Shack Off PSP Geese"

A shack beside the PSP — the perforated steel matting laid down for a temporary airstrip.

#79
"Grew Chief, .30 Cal."

A crew chief at a mounted .30-caliber machine gun — a door gun position.

#80
"H-21 Spectators"

An H-21 Shawnee on the strip draws a small crowd of onlookers.

#81
"Loading"

The H-21 up close, being loaded.

#82
"Civil Denfense Corps (Hah!)"

Two figures along a fence line. The parenthetical is his — his own aside, not ours.

#83
"Losers"

A Vietnamese cemetery behind a wire fence — traditional stone grave markers. Dark humor.

#84
"PSP."

The airstrip matting itself, empty.

#85
"Otter, L-19 L-19"

#86
"Shack"

Open field, tree line, a small structure.

#88

A Red Cross flag over a tent — Dr. Ralph sitting among the soldiers gathered around it, very possibly his own aid station.

#89

Literal — a rig with hooks for hanging gear. Behind it, UH-1 Hueys marked "ARMY."

#90

Named directly.

#91

A C-123 Provider bringing in supplies.

#92
"CH-37"

A CH-37 Mojave with a CH-21 Shawnee behind it. Cross-referenced against a second CH-37 photo from a different tray, captioned "Off To Rescue" — likely this aircraft's actual job here.

#93
"Beggers"

Vietnamese children near the base, seated along a rail. His word, kept as he wrote it.

#94
"Camp"

A sheltered structure under trees — reads more like a local dwelling than a U.S. position, but not certain.

Núi Bà Đen, at dusk. This is what places the whole tray in Tay Ninh.

#100
