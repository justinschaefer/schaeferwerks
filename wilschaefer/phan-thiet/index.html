Phan Thiet, "Green Pastures"

SP4 WIL SCHAEFER · 45TH MED DET · DATE UNCONFIRMED

A troop-insertion mission by CH-21, shot from the door gun position and the air: the approach over Phan Thiet, landing with troops, loading them back up, an Otter recon pass, and the same CH-37 "Off To Rescue" that appears in the Tay Ninh tray. Slide 70 and 74 were never scanned, or didn't survive.

#81
"VIP"

A twin-engine aircraft in a distinctive red-and-white paint scheme, landing — consistent with a Beechcraft U-8 Seminole, the Army's staff/VIP transport of the era. "U.S. Army 23636" is legible on the tail. Also visible: two CH-21s on the ground, tail numbers 62091 (left) and a third example of the same numbering cluster seen elsewhere in this tray (62095, 62100).

#82
"CH-37 Off To Rescue"

A CH-37 Mojave in flight, cargo hook visibly hanging beneath the fuselage — direct physical evidence it was rigged for a hoist or sling recovery, not just flying past. Two more CH-21s visible on the ground below. This is the same caption cross-referenced from the Tay Ninh tray — confirmation this aircraft's role in his unit's area was heavy-lift rescue work.
