Tan Son Nhut

SP4 WIL SCHAEFER · 45TH MED DET · DATE UNCONFIRMED

This is home base — not a mission or a trip, but where he actually lived and worked. His bunk, his dispensary, the unit's mascot monkey, a memorial for the first man in the unit killed in Vietnam, and Dr. Ralph — the same man from the Rach Gia tray, here as a working colleague, not just someone he met once. It also holds the single strangest sequence in the whole collection: twenty consecutive frames of a cockfight he called a "Turkey Fight." Slides 30 and 64 were never scanned, or didn't survive.
